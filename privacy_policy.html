Privacy Policy – MyChild

Effective Date: May 2026

MyChild is designed to help parents organize and track their child's wellness information including vaccines, growth records, meal plans and more.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use MyChild.

Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Authentication information through Google Sign-In or phone authentication

Child Wellness Information

  • Child name and profile details
  • Date of birth
  • Vaccine records
  • Growth entries (height, weight, head circumference)

How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to provide the MyChild app features. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising purposes.

Data Storage

All data is stored securely using Google Firebase. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
